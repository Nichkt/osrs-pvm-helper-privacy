Privacy policy

OSRS PvM Helper

Effective 18 November 2026

Summary

OSRS PvM Helper does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party data collection SDKs. The app does not request or store personal information.

Data stored on the device

The search query and selected boss remain only in temporary application memory. The app bundles its boss and equipment catalog in the installation and can be used without signing in.

Network requests

The app uses an internet connection to request boss and item artwork from the Old School RuneScape Wiki. The web version also embeds the official OSRS Wiki DPS calculator when the user opens a calculator section. Those services receive normal network information such as an IP address as part of delivering their pages and images, subject to their own privacy policies.

Children's data

OSRS PvM Helper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other user.

Contact

Contact the developer using the support email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
